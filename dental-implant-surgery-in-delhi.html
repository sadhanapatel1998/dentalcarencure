Understanding Guided Dental Implant Surgery

Computer-guided dental implant surgery is an intricate process of implant placement through digital planning, 3D CBCT scan, and computer navigation to find the best placement, angle, and depth of the implants even before the surgery starts.

This advanced approach offers higher precision and proper placement of implants. This advanced method is usually advised for patients who need to receive single-tooth implants, multi-tooth implants, and full mouth rehabilitation.

Symptoms That May Indicate You Need Guided Dental Implant Surgery

You may benefit from guided dental implant surgery in Delhi if you experience:

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting food properly
  • Gaps in your smile affecting your appearance
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Shifting of neighbouring teeth after tooth loss
  • Lowered self-confidence when talking or smiling

If any of these signs are present, you may seek a dental examination to find out if guided dental implant surgery in Delhi is right for you.

Guided Dental Implant Surgery in Delhi - Treatment Options at Dental Care N Cure

The treatment of choice for implants will be decided on the total number of missing teeth, the status of the jawbone, and general dental health. At Dental Care N Cure, proceed with personalised treatment planning. Our wide range of digital dental implant treatment options includes:

  • Single Tooth Dental Implant: This type of implant can be very useful in cases where you have one missing tooth. This implant not only allows you to chew normally but keeps your jawbone in good shape and helps to install Implant Supported Crowns.
  • Dental Implants: For those patients who have lost several teeth, the use of dental implants would be beneficial. They will give you solid support for your replacement teeth and protect your bone structure.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge: In case you have lost several teeth in the same proximity, you need to go for an implant-supported bridge.
  • Full Mouth dental implants: If a full arch of teeth needs to be replaced with dental implants for multiple missing teeth, the one arch is used for all teeth.
  • All-on-4 dental implants: All-on-4 dental implants consist of a set of teeth attached to 4 implants.
  • Bone Grafting & Dental Implants: In case one lacks jawbone density for dental implants, bone grafting may be done to make space for the implants.

Gums play an important role in ensuring success with dental implants, and thus, those who have problems with their gums might need Gum Disease Treatment in Delhi prior to the procedure.
