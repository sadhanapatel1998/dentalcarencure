What Are Dental Veneers?

ThVeneers are small ceramic dental restorations that are attached to the front surface of the teeth, thus enhancing the teeth aesthetically. Veneers are used to improve the form, color, size of teeth and teeth reshaping. Other aesthetic problems, such as discoloration, chipped, and misshapen teeth, can be improved by veneers. Porcelain or composite materials are used to create veneers in order to match real teeth in durability and natural appearance.

Who Needs Dental Veneers?

Some of the cases where dental veneers might be considered include people with:

  • Teeth stained or discolored and not corrected by whitening
  • Cracked or chipped teeth
  • Spaces between the teeth
  • Irregularly shaped teeth
  • Slightly crooked teeth

If you are facing any of these issues, then taking an expert opinion regarding dental veneers in Delhi will help you ascertain if you are a good candidate for veneers to enhance your smile.

Types of Dental Veneers in Delhi We offer

Dental Care N Cure is trusted dental clinic in Delhi that provides different types of dental veneers in Delhi based on your smile goals, tooth condition, and aesthetic requirements. Our dentists recommend the most suitable veneer option after a detailed evaluation.

  • Porcelain Veneers: Porcelain veneer is a thin slices of porcelain used to improve the appearance of the teeth and make them look natural. The porcelain veneers treatment can stand up to staining and wear well.
  • Composite Veneers: Composite veneers are made up of dental resin material, which is applied on the teeth to address small problems like discoloration of teeth, chipping of teeth, and misshapen teeth.
  • No-Prep Veneers: No-prep veneers require the removal of very little enamel from the teeth as opposed to the regular veneers. In some cases, no-prep veneers can be good for flawless smile.

Choosing the right cosmetic dental veneers for your teeth will depend on your condition and your dentist’s advice.

Dental Veneers Treatment Procedure

Dental veneers in Delhi at Dental Care N Cure are done in accordance with your dental condition, smiling objectives, and aesthetic needs.

  • Examination and consultation: The dentist checks up on your teeth, hears you out, and decides if you are a veneer candidate for the smile you want.
  • Smile Planning:Your personal smile planning is done based on your teeth color, shape, size, face form, and smile proportions.
  • Teeth Preparation:There might be a minor reduction of the enamel layer at the front side of your teeth for the installation of the veneers.
  • Veneers Making:The dentist makes the veneers using impressions that allow for a natural look.
  • Veneers Bonding:The dentist installs the veneers and makes their fitting and comfortable adjustment.
  • Final Smile Examination:The dentist assesses the result and checks how good they are in terms of function and aesthetics.
