What Is TMJ Disorder?

The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) is where the lower part of your jaw is joined to the skull and by this joint you are able to chew and talk as well as yawn. The inflammation of the joint, its misalignment and stress may result in TMJ disorder.

    Common symptoms include:
  • Jaw pain or tenderness
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Clicking or popping sounds
  • Difficulty chewing
  • Headaches or ear pain
  • Locking of the jaw

Timely Treatment of TMJ disorder in Paschim Vihar is significant to avoid the complications in the long term. TMJ pains are confused with the normal toothache or the sinus pains, something that postpones appropriate diagnosis.

What Causes TMJ Disorder?

The knowledge of the cause will assist in identifying the appropriate TMJ disorder Treatment in Paschim Vihar. The TMJ disorders can be caused by:

  • Teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Jaw injury or trauma
  • Arthritis in the joint
  • Misaligned bite
  • Chronic stress

There are cases when several factors are used jointly. As an illustration, the joint may be strained under the pressure of stemming out clenching due to stress, eventually resulting in inflammation.
