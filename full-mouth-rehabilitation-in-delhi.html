Treatment Options Included in Full Mouth Rehabilitation

The procedures recommended in full mouth rehabilitation in Delhi depend on your specific dental condition and treatment objectives.

  • Dental Implants: Dental implants are devices which allow missing teeth replacement with stable and durable options for chewing ability and cosmetic purposes.
  • Dental Crowns: These are useful in restoring the functionality of damaged or weakened teeth. The crowns strengthen the remaining parts of the teeth.
  • Dental Bridges: These too can be used as replacements for a single tooth or multiple missing teeth. This will help restore functionality and alignment of other teeth.
  • Bite correction: In case of an irregular bite, it might lead to wear and tear, pain, and other functional issues. Herein comes the role of bite correction.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry: Cosmetic procedures may be carried out to make sure the teeth look good.
  • Treatment for Gum Diseases: Having healthy gums is key to successfully treating any dental issue. Gum diseases must be addressed in case of need for better oral hygiene.

One can also explore therapies like Dental Veneers in Delhi can help improve the look, color, and overall beauty of the teeth.
