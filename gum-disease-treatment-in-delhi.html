Understanding Gum Disease

Gum disease is a mouth infection that affects your gums and the bone surrounding your teeth. The irritants of bacteria and plaque that collect on the gums make the gums swollen and cause gum disease.Gum bleeding and inflammation occur in the initial phase of gum disease. It may further progress to cause the destruction of bone and supporting tissues in the mouth, thus causing loosening of teeth and loss of teeth.

Our expert gum infection doctor in Delhi at Dental Care N Cure offers customised treatment for your periodontal problems after assessing the extent of the problem and offering appropriate gum disease treatment in Delhi.

Symptoms That May Indicate Gum Disease

  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gum pain and sensitivity
  • Bleeding gums when you brush or floss
  • Red or inflamed gums
  • Gum recession causing teeth to appear longer
  • Loose teeth
  • Pain while chewing
  • Increased gaps between teeth
  • Pus or signs of infection
  • Bite changes/tooth movement

If you have noticed these signs and symptoms, then a visit to a gum specialist in Delhi will surely help you. Early detection and timely gum disease treatment in Delhi can stop the disease progression and save you from permanent oral damage.

Types and Stages of Gum Disease

There are two stages when it comes to the development of gum disease:

  • Gingivitis: Early stage of gum disease (gingivitis), which develops when tartar accumulates beneath the gums. This causes inflammation, redness, and bleeding during toothbrushing of the gums. Then, with good cleaning and better daily care, nothing at this stage of the condition is too difficult to treat.
  • Periodontitis: It is an advanced stage of gum disease that develops due to ignoring gingivitis or not getting it treated in time. It involves issues like gum recession, pocket formation, tooth loosening, and tooth loss.

An advanced periodontal disease treatment in Delhi may comprise deep cleaning and laser treatment of the gums.

Diagnosis of Gum Disease

Before starting treatment, our dentists perform a detailed evaluation to understand the condition of your gums. Diagnosis may include:

  • Checking gum inflammation and bleeding
  • Evaluating plaque and tartar buildup
  • Measuring gum pocket depth
  • Assessing tooth stability

According to the examination, a customized gum disease treatment in Delhi is designed for gum health improvement and to prevent any more damage.

During the examination, our dentists also assess overall oral function, bite alignment, and related concerns that may contribute to discomfort. Patients experiencing jaw-related symptoms may sometimes require further evaluation for TMJ Treatment in Delhi.
