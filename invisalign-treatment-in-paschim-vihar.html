What Are Invisible Braces?

Invisible braces are the new, transparent aligners that not only straighten teeth but also do so discreetly, unlike traditional metal braces. These clear trays are handmade to fit your teeth perfectly and apply gentle and controlled pressure to move them into the desired position bit by bit. At Dental Care N Cure, we offer Invisalign treatment in Paschim Vihar that is comfortable, removable, and nearly impossible to notice.

    Why are invisible braces popular?

  • Almost Unnoticeable: Clear aligners, besides being invisible, are also super discreet, hence nobody will even think of guessing that you are undergoing a dental treatment for teeth straightening. They do not change the original look, thus letting the smile be self-assured and attractive during the whole treatment.
  • Faster & Predicted results: The movements of the teeth are precisely monitored with the help of state-of-the-art digital treatment planning. This implies that the teeth shift very quickly to the target spot, thus resulting in getting faster results that are estimable and of assured quality.
  • Removable and Easy to Use: The aligners are designed for easy removal during meals, teeth cleaning, or other related situations. The aligner allows you to continue your daily routine, maintain good oral hygiene, and all this without any impact on your orthodontic progress.
  • Non-irritating Treatment: The custom trays made of smooth and perfectly fit to your mouth will never cause irritation or discomfort because there are no sharp edges, brackets, or wires involved. In the same manner, Invisaligns are very gentle and do not cause any discomfort; they get used to being in your mouth like traditional braces.
  • Child-Friendly Dentistry: Children undergo a special process where the entire process of methods is painless to remove their fear and to build up good dental habits.
  • All Age suitability: Smiling teeth can be a great way to relieve the stress of kids, adults, and working people. If not, invisible braces are less likely to be noticed. At the same time, they give effective results even if they do not disturb the patient's daily life and appearance very much.
