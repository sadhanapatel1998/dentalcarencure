What Are Kids' Braces?

Kids' braces are orthodontic devices used to align the teeth and fix dental disorders among growing kids. They help in shifting the teeth to their rightful place while also helping with proper jaw alignment. Kids braces in Delhi are quite popularly chosen for cases of overcrowding of teeth, misalignment of teeth, overbites, spacing issues, underbites, etc.

Signs Your Child May Need Braces

Not all orthodontic conditions may become apparent to the parents at an early stage. Nevertheless, some warning signs could point out whether or not your child needs orthodontic treatment.

The common warning signs include:

  • Crooked or overlapping teeth
  • Crowded teeth with limited space
  • Obvious spacing in teeth
  • Trouble chewing or biting food
  • Overlapping front teeth
  • Jaw alignment concerns

These are some of the signs and symptoms that indicate that an orthodontic evaluation should be done to see whether the child requires braces.

Why is Early Orthodontic Evaluation Important?

Through early intervention, the dentist will be able to find out if there are any abnormalities in the formation of teeth. These problems can be corrected at the right time and help in the development of jawbones.

An expert pediatric orthodontist in Delhi is able to develop a plan of care for the healthy growth of the teeth and improvement of their function through early orthodontic treatment. Early diagnosis of kids braces in Delhi may be helpful to diagnose orthodontic problems before they become serious enough to cope with.

Sometimes, very serious bite disorders and alignment problems that remain unattended in the early stage of life might lead to heavy dental wear later in life and require specialized worn teeth treatment in Delhi.

Types of Braces Available for Children

At Dental Care N Cure, we offer different orthodontic solutions based on your child's age, dental condition, and treatment requirements. The right option for kids braces in Delhi depends on your child's age, dental development, and orthodontic requirements.

  • Metal Braces: Metal braces work really well to fix misaligned teeth, overcrowding of teeth, and jaw alignment issues. They are some of the most popular kinds to be recommended to children.
  • Ceramic Braces: Just like the metal braces, the ceramic braces function the same way, but with one major difference in that their brackets are white.
  • Self-Ligating Braces: This kind of brace uses special brackets that may reduce friction during the process of adjustment.
  • Clear Aligners for Selected Cases: For older kids and teens with some specific orthodontic issues, clear aligners can be used.

Braces for Children in Delhi at Dental Care N Cure: Working Procedure

  • Step 1: Orthodontic Consultation: The orthodontist will examine your child’s teeth, bite, and jaw alignment to assess if any of these are abnormal and need attention.
  • Step 2: Treatment Planning: Based on the evaluation, a personalised treatment plan is created to determine the most suitable type of braces and treatment approach.
  • Step 3: Brackets Placement: Careful attachment of the brackets to the teeth is followed by the placement of an orthodontic wire for teeth alignment.
  • Step 4: Carefully Moving the Teeth: The teeth are pushed to their correct place in the mouth by pressure exerted on them by braces.
  • Step 5: Follow-Up and Adjustments: At the follow-up, they may also make the wires tighter to assist with moving the teeth.
  • Step 6: Straightening the Teeth: The use of braces eventually results in aligning and correcting any misalignments or bite problems.
  • Step 7: Removal of Braces and Use of Retainers: There comes a time, once teeth have been successfully aligned, that braces need to be removed, and retainers are used.

With this method, the child ends up with better-aligned teeth.
