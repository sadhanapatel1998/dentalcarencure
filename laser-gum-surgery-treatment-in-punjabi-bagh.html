The Need for Gum Surgery & Laser Gum Treatment

  • Persistent Gum Bleeding: Usual bleeding during brushing or flossing is often a sign of gum infection or the initial stage of periodontal disease needing professional help.
  • Gum recession: The gums retracting from the teeth suggests that the roots of the teeth are getting more and more open, their sensitivity, in turn, is increased, and the probability of infection spreading is thus greater.
  • Constant bad breath: A surgical or laser therapy is needed due to a deep gum infection could be one of the reasons for the persistent bad breath or bad taste in the mouth.
  • Tooth mobility or bite changes: The gum tissue has worn away so much that it cannot hold a tooth, and this is a situation that needs immediate dental care so as not to let the damage spread further.

Gum Treatment Options Available at Dental Care & Cure

  • Advanced Technology & Modern Setup: Professional Scaling & Root Planing: A systematic cleaning treatment that takes away all the plaque, tartar, and germs that have been mounting up under the gum line completely. One can then effectively deal with gum infections, decrease the swelling, and prevent further problems.
  • Laser Gum Treatment: This surgery uses the highest-grade laser technology that accurately focuses on the diseased gum tissue while the healthy ones are left alone. This not only makes the whole process less bloody and painful, but faster healing too.
  • Preventive Gum Care: It includes regular dental check-ups, professional cleaning, and personalized gum surgery treatment in Punjabi Bagh to prevent the occurrence of future gum problems and also to promote long-term oral health.
  • Periodontal Surgery: This is for advanced gum disease, eliminates deep periodontal pockets, takes away the severely infected tissue, and aids in the restoration of the gums' health when non-surgical treatments have failed.
  • Gum Reshaping & Contouring: Corrects the issue of unequal distribution or surplus of gum tissue to not only enhance the aesthetics of the smile but also make the oral hygiene easier, thus reducing the accumulation of plaque and bacteria.

Why Choose Laser Gum Treatment

Laser gum treatment is a modern technique that is safe and highly effective for treating different gum diseases, and has many advantages over traditional surgeries. Using lasers made the whole process minimally invasive and very precise in the affected gum area treatment while leaving the healthy parts untouched; therefore, the outcome is reduced bleeding, swelling, and pain. A faster healing process is experienced by the patients, and they can go back to their normal activities earlier than they would have if they had conventional gum surgery.

Moreover, the laser that is utilized in the procedure will be able to kill the bacteria that are found in the area, thus, the risk of infection will be eliminated, which will lead to safer and more dependable results.
