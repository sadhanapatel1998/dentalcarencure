What is orthodontics ?

High Quality Cost Effective Treatment

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that deals with the diagnosis, preventions and corrections of dental and facial irregularities occurring as a result of,

  1. A bad bite.
  2. Missing of Extra Teeth.
  3. Misaligned teeth.
  4. Crowed teeth.
  5. An Overbite.

At what age can one start orthodontic treatment?

Early Childhood –

You can have an orthodontic screening from the age of 7, by which time enough of the permanent teeth would have emerged, helping to identify potential problems. Do not wait for the all the permanent teeth to erupt in the mouth, as the earlier you start the more the advantages.

During Adolescent and Teenage years-

All permanent teeth would have come in by now and treatment is most effective at this age.

At Adulthood –

Healthy teeth can be moved at any age. orthodontic treatment can help create and restore good function. Teeth that work better usually look better, too.
