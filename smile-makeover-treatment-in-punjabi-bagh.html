At Dental Care N Cure, our smile makeover treatment in Punjabi Bagh not only beautifies the smile but also helps with oral hygiene while giving a confidence boost to the patient. The complete treatment comprises:

  • Dental Veneers: A very thin layer of porcelain or composite resin made in a dental lab is applied to the tooth in order to conceal the flaws. The flaws may include chipped, irregular, or discolored teeth, thus making the whole area look pretty and natural.
