The days of having to endure metal braces just to have a nice and aligned smile are gone. Dental Care N Cure offers pain-free invisible teeth aligners treatment in Paschim Vihar that gradually straightens the teeth without any interruption in daily life. The aligner therapy has become the most popular and the most comfortable option, which yields a confident change regardless of the corrections' nature—closing gaps, fixing bites, or simply improving the look of your teeth.

Benefits of Clear Aligners:

  • Almost invisible, the orthodontic option is extremely discreet
  • Trays are soft and non-irritating, and there are also no wires or brackets
  • The removable design makes eating, brushing & flossing effortless
  • Digital treatment planning leads to quicker and more certain results
  • Reduction in the number of clinic visits and lifestyle interruptions that are small

Who Can Get Aligners?

Our teeth aligners treatment in Paschim Vihar that are invisible is a perfect option for both youngsters and grown-ups who want a comfortable and invisible way of styling their teeth. If you have the following issue, you might be a good candidate:
  • Crowding that is slightly visible to moderately visible
  • Separation or blank spaces between your teeth
  • Misaligned or wrongly positioned teeth
  • Overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Teeth movement after previous orthodontic treatment

    • Teeth aligners treatment in Paschim Vihar is most effective for people who are devoted to wearing them for the suggested hours each day and are also very careful with their oral hygiene.
