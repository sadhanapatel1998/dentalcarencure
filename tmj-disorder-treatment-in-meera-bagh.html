Understanding TMJ disorder

Temporomandibular Joint Disorder is a disease that leads to the inflammation of the jaw joints and thus causes pain in the daily activities of eating, speaking, and yawning.

    The list of symptoms consists of:
  • Pain or tenderness in or near the jaw area: It is the discomfort that may become worse with jaw movements.
  • Click, pop, or grating: This is the sound associated with the opening of the mouth or the sound associated with the closure of the mouth.
  • Headaches or ear pain: Pain from the jaw going to the temple area and then to the ear region.
  • Difficulty in chewing or restricted movement of jaws or jaw muscles;Opening or closing jaws with difficulty.

The conditions of the temporomandibular joint might arise in the event that a person has tooth grinding, improper occlusion, stress, and trauma.
