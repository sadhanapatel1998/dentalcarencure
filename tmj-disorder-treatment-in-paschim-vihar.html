TMJ disorder causes

  • Bruising (Teeth Grinding and Clenching): The jaw muscles produce "friction" to the point of ultimately feeling fatigued and sore.
  • Jaw Injury or Trauma: Apart from accidents such as car crashes, the jaw joint can be subjected to injuries during falls or sports activities
  • Arthritis: Both osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis can lead to acute suffering of the jaw joint, and, in extremely severe cases, even destruction of the joint may become a consequence.
  • Stress and Muscle Tension: Stress as a constant demand could be too much for the jaw to take, and thus the result is the clenching, the tightness, and finally the pain signal.
  • Misaligned Bite (Malocclusion): Misalignment of the occlusion leads to a dental problem that prevents the joint from moving freely because the teeth or jaws are misaligned in relation to the joint.
  • Habitual Movements: Smoking, gum chewing, biting nails, etc., are considered common and often unrecognized daily habit movements of the jaw.

    • The detection of the root problem becomes a necessity since it will be the foundation for the personalized and efficient TMJ disorder treatment in Paschim Vihar.

TMJ disorder: Symptoms and Signs
  • Jaw Pain or Discomfort: An area of pain or discomfort not just in the joint, perhaps a bit over the head and even up to the ear canal.
  • Chewing or Biting Difficulties: A small area of pain in the mouth during the process of chewing or biting.
  • <4strong>Clicking or Popping Noises: Sometimes the noises are heard; at the same time, the jaw movements could be associated with slight pain.
  • Jaw Movement Difficulty: The individual may have problems with broad opening and closing of the mouth, or even be in a situation of being unable to open the mouth at all.
  • Headaches or Discomfort in the Face: Chronic headache, earache, or muscle weariness of the face could be experienced.
  • Gradual or Daily Clenching of Teeth: This often leads to exacerbation of the symptoms, and in severe cases, it can result in the total collapse of teeth due to erosion.
